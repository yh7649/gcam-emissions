# South Korea power-sector EF source documents

These source documents were migrated from NZK-APHIAM on 2026-09-08. Exact
citations, source URLs, local filenames, and access status are recorded in
`sources.csv`; file checksums and extracted coverage are recorded one directory
above in `literature_pdf_inventory.csv`.

The collection contains:

- Seo, Kim, and Jeon (2019), oil and bio-heavy-oil output-factor methodology;
- Lee et al. (2025), 2022 coal-complex generation and pollutant mass;
- KEEI (2017), 2016 power-sector particulate-reduction analysis;
- MOTIE (2019), national coal/LNG air-pollutant comparisons;
- Yu et al. (2021), load-specific Korean coal measurements normalized by fuel;
- Solutions for Our Climate, contextual biomass evidence; and
- the official 2025 CAPSS Handbook VII.

These PDFs are third-party research inputs, not project outputs, and their
presence does not grant redistribution rights. The CAPSS manual is the default
local source for `make scrape-capss`; use `make scrape-capss-verified` to
require a byte-for-byte match to the current official attachment.

The archive also records web-only or unavailable-full-text leads in
`sources.csv`. No numeric value is inferred from an abstract or a source that
could not be reviewed.
